Adaptive Communication-Constrained DSGF for Efficient UAV Swarm Coordination

**Dear Editor,**

We submit “Adaptive Communication-Constrained DSGF for Efficient UAV Swarm
Coordination” to *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*.

Communication efficiency is a critical challenge for large-scale UAV swarm
systems. Existing multi-agent reinforcement learning approaches usually rely on
fixed or dense communication structures, leading to unnecessary communication
overhead and limiting applicability under bandwidth-, energy-, and
latency-constrained airborne networks.

In this work, we propose **AC-DSGF**, a communication-aware graph coordination
framework that treats **communication topology selection as a learnable decision
process**. The proposed framework integrates:
(1) **adaptive communication topology learning**,
(2) **communication budget optimization**, and
(3) **residual guidance for stable sparse coordination**.

Experiments with up to 16 UAV agents demonstrate that AC-DSGF maintains
**comparable task performance** while reducing communication cost by
**approximately two orders of magnitude**, supported by budget Pareto analysis,
ablations, communication-behavior diagnostics, and packet-loss tests.

The proposed method provides a practical solution for
**communication-constrained UAV swarm coordination** and aligns with RA-L’s
interest in scalable multi-robot systems and deployable autonomy.
