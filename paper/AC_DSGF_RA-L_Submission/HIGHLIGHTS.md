# Highlights — AC-DSGF (IEEE RA-L · FINAL)
# 4 bullets · claim-safe · no “90× improvement” phrasing

---

### H1
A communication-aware graph learning framework is proposed for UAV swarm
coordination under limited bandwidth.

### H2
Communication topology is formulated as a learnable decision variable instead
of a predefined graph.

### H3
A budget-aware communication mechanism reduces unnecessary interactions while
preserving task effectiveness.

### H4
Extensive experiments with 16 UAV agents validate communication–performance
trade-offs under constrained messaging
(approximately two orders of magnitude lower communication overhead at
comparable task performance).
