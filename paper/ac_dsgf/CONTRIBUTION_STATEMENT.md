# Contribution Statement — AC-DSGF v1（RA-L style, locked）

## Title
Learning Adaptive Topologies for Communication-Constrained UAV Swarm Coordination

## Method
**AC-DSGF** (Adaptive Constraint-aware Dynamic Sparse Graph Framework), v1

---

## Contribution 1
> We formulate adaptive communication topology learning as a joint optimization problem between swarm performance and communication cost.

## Contribution 2
> We propose a differentiable topology adaptation mechanism with budget regularization and residual guidance that jointly considers task relevance and communication budgets (Candidate Edge Scoring → Budget-Constrained Edge Selection → Residual Recovery).

## Contribution 3
> We validate that the proposed method achieves comparable coordination performance while substantially reducing communication activation (SCA) under different swarm scales and budgeted deployments.

---

## Positioning (one sentence)
> Communication topology is elevated from fixed network infrastructure to a UAV-swarm decision variable under soft budget constraints.

## Out of contribution scope
SwarmOS, PX4, engineering runtime, real deployment system claims.
