# Cover Letter — IEEE RA-L（AC-DSGF v1）

**Manuscript title:**  
Learning Adaptive Topologies for Communication-Constrained UAV Swarm Coordination

**Dear Editor,**

We submit the above manuscript to *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*.

Existing multi-UAV policies often treat communication topology as **fixed infrastructure**, while attention weights improve aggregation but do not decide link **activation**. We study *when and with whom* UAVs should communicate under soft budgets, formulate joint optimization of task performance and communication activation, and propose **AC-DSGF**—a differentiable topology adaptation mechanism with budget regularization and residual guidance. We provide a degree-constrained scaling interpretation and show **comparable coordination with substantially lower Soft Communication Activation (SCA)** under identical / budgeted deployments.

The work targets communication-constrained multi-robot coordination and aligns with RA-L’s scope.

Sincerely,  
[Authors]

**Keywords:** adaptive communication topology; soft budget constraints; UAV swarm; communication activation; multi-agent RL
