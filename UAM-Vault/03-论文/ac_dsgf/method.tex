\section{Method: AC-DSGF}
\label{sec:method}

We propose \emph{Adaptive Communication-Constrained Dynamic Sparse Graph Fusion}
(AC-DSGF), a communication-aware graph coordination framework that treats
interaction topology as a \textbf{learnable decision variable}.
Unlike post-hoc message pruning, AC-DSGF jointly learns the control policy and
a task-dependent sparse communication structure under an explicit soft
activation cost.
Fig.~\ref{fig:framework} shows three layers:
(i)~observation embedding,
(ii)~adaptive topology learning (gate + budget),
(iii)~residual-guided coordination.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.98\linewidth]{figures/framework.png}
  \caption{Framework of AC-DSGF: adaptive topology learning (gate + budget)
  defines the interaction graph consumed by residual DSGF guidance, rather
  than post-hoc pruning of a fixed graph.}
  \label{fig:framework}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:joint}

Let $r_t=\sum_i r_i^{t}$ denote the team reward at time $t$ and
\begin{equation}
C_t
=
\sum_{i\neq j}
g_{ij}^{t}
\label{eq:Ct}
\end{equation}
the Soft Communication Activation Mass (soft intensity proxy; not a physical
packet count).
AC-DSGF optimizes
\begin{equation}
\max_{\theta}
\;
\mathbb{E}\!\Biggl[
\sum_{t=0}^{H}
r_t
\Biggr]
-
\lambda_c\,
\mathbb{E}\!\Biggl[
\sum_{t=0}^{H}
C_t
\Biggr],
\label{eq:joint-max}
\end{equation}
i.e., topology intensity is optimized \emph{together with} policy learning.
This differs from training a dense communicator and then pruning messages at
deployment time.

\subsection{Adaptive Communication Topology Learning}
\label{sec:ac-graph}

\paragraph{Feasible mask and quality.}
The radius mask $A_{ij}^{t}=\mathbb{1}[\|p_i^{t}-p_j^{t}\|<R_c]$ and quality
$q_{ij}^{t}\in(0,1)$ restrict candidate edges to the geometric support.

\paragraph{Gate (learned selectivity).}
Let $h_i^{t}=\psi(o_i^{t})$ and $d_{ij}^{t}$ the pairwise distance.
The communication gate is
\begin{equation}
g_{ij}^{t}
=
\sigma\!\Bigl(
W_g
\bigl[
h_i^{t};\,
h_j^{t};\,
d_{ij}^{t};\,
q_{ij}^{t}
\bigr]
\Bigr)
\cdot
A_{ij}^{t}
\in[0,1],
\label{eq:gate}
\end{equation}
where $W_g$ denotes a lightweight MLP.
Task conditioning enters primarily through node embeddings $h_i$ (goal and
state information); unlike random dropout, $g_{ij}^{t}$ is a learned function
of agent states on the radius support
(Sec.~\ref{sec:trigger}, Sec.~\ref{sec:gate-dist}).

\paragraph{Difference from pruning.}
Post-hoc pruning removes links after (or independently of) policy learning.
AC-DSGF changes the \emph{information topology} inside the forward pass:
setting $g{=}A$ (``no-budget'') restores near-full local broadcast and
inflates Soft Comm.\ Mass by over three orders of magnitude with only a small
Success change (Sec.~\ref{sec:silence}).
Hence AC-DSGF performs \textbf{topology optimization}, not merely message
pruning.

\subsection{Communication Budget Optimization}
\label{sec:budget}

Given budget ratio $\rho\in(0,1]$,
\begin{equation}
K_i=\lceil\rho\,|\mathcal{N}_i|\rceil,
\qquad
E_t^{K}
=
\mathrm{TopK}_{K_i}\!\bigl(\{g_{ij}^{t}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}_i}\bigr),
\label{eq:topk}
\end{equation}
and the adaptive communication adjacency
\begin{equation}
A_t^{\mathrm{AC}}
=
A^{t}
\odot
E_t^{K},
\qquad
e_{ij}^{t}
=
A_{ij}^{\mathrm{AC}}\,
q_{ij}^{t}\,
g_{ij}^{t}.
\label{eq:Aac}
\end{equation}
Training may use soft $g$ with the cost in Eq.~\eqref{eq:joint-max};
deployment can enforce hard Top-$K$ without retraining
(Sec.~\ref{sec:topk-deploy}).

\subsection{Residual-Guided Coordination}
\label{sec:residual}

Sparse attention aggregates neighbors under $e^{t}$:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{ij}^{t}
\propto
\exp\!\bigl(f(h_i^{t},h_j^{t})\bigr)\,e_{ij}^{t},
\qquad
h_i^{\prime\,t}
=
\sigma\!\Bigl(
\sum_{j}
\alpha_{ij}^{t} W h_j^{t}
\Bigr),
\label{eq:attn}
\end{equation}
followed by $z_i^{t}=\mathrm{GRU}(h_i^{\prime\,t},z_i^{t-1})$ and guidance
$\Phi_i^{t}$.
Actions use residual decoupling
\begin{equation}
a_i^{t}
\sim
\pi_{\theta}\!\bigl(\cdot\mid o_i^{t}\bigr)
\;+\;
\beta(t)\,
\Delta\!\bigl(\Phi_i^{t}\bigr),
\label{eq:residual}
\end{equation}
so coordination \emph{assists} rather than dominates execution
(Table~\ref{tab:ablation}: $9.27\%\!\rightarrow\!0.22\%$ without residual).

\subsection{Gate Stability (non-random switching)}
\label{sec:gate-stable}

A potential concern is that sparse gates might chatter and destabilize training.
Using frozen AC-DSGF rollouts, we measure soft-mass variance $\mathrm{Var}(C_t)$ and
the edge-change rate
\begin{equation}
\Delta E_t
=
\bigl|\,|E_t|-|E_{t-1}|\,\bigr|.
\label{eq:deltaE}
\end{equation}
Empirically (Fig.~\ref{fig:gate-stable}; 12 episodes),
$\mathrm{Var}(C_t){\approx}3\times10^{-3}$ and
${>}99\%$ of steps have $\Delta E_t{=}0$: $C_t$ varies smoothly with the
task phase while edges rarely chatter.
Communication adapts when proximity risk changes (Sec.~\ref{sec:trigger}),
rather than randomly toggling.
