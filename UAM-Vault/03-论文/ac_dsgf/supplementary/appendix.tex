\section*{S1. Network architecture (summary)}
AC-DSGF wraps a spatial--temporal graph encoder with a differentiable gate head.
Node embedding dimension $d_h{=}128$; spatial layers use communication-aware
sparse attention (2 layers, 4 heads); residual guidance dimension $d_\Phi{=}6$.
Gates $g_{ij}=\sigma(s_{ij})\cdot A_{ij}$ are restricted to the radius support
$A_{ij}$ ($R_c{=}0.5$). Soft training cost $C_s=\sum g_{ij}$ (SCAM) is
penalized by $\lambda_c$ in $\mathbb{E}[R-\lambda_c C_s]$. Hard deployment uses
thresholding or per-agent Top-$K$.

\section*{S3. Proposition~1 derivation (degree constraint)}
Assume $|E_t(i)|\le K$ for each agent. Then $|E_t|\le NK$.
The complete digraph has $|E_{\mathrm{full}}|=N(N-1)$, hence
\[
\eta_N=\frac{|E_t|}{|E_{\mathrm{full}}|}\le\frac{K}{N-1}=\mathcal{O}\!\left(\frac1N\right).
\]
This is a \emph{scaling interpretation under bounded neighborhood}, not a
learning-convergence claim and not an equality $|E_t|=KN$.
