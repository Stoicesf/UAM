\subsection{Computational and Communication Complexity}
\label{sec:complexity}

Let $N$ be the number of UAVs, $d$ the hidden width, $\mathcal{N}_i$ the
feasible neighbors of agent $i$ under radius $R_c$, and
$k=\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_i|\mathcal{N}_i|$ the average degree ($k\ll N$ in sparse
swarms).
Let $K_i=\lceil\rho|\mathcal{N}_i|\rceil$ denote the Top-$K$ budgeted degree
and $K=\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_i K_i$ the average budgeted degree ($K\le k$).

\paragraph{Dense / GAT baselines.}
Full pairwise messaging or dense attention costs
$\mathcal{O}(N^{2}d)$ per layer.
Even with a binary radius mask, GAT-style aggregation that treats all
in-radius neighbors equally still processes $\Theta(Nk)$ edges; under moderate
$R_c$ this rapidly approaches quadratic growth in $N$.

\paragraph{AC-DSGF.}
Gate evaluation runs on the feasible edge set:
$\mathcal{O}(|E|d)=\mathcal{O}(Nkd)$.
Top-$K$ budgeting per agent costs $\mathcal{O}(k\log k)$ (or
$\mathcal{O}(K\log k)$ with partial selection), totaling
$\mathcal{O}(Nk\log k)$.
Sparse attention then operates on the budgeted support of size $\mathcal{O}(NK)$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{O}\!\bigl(NKd + Nd\bigr)
\quad\text{(attention + GRU)},
\label{eq:ac-complexity}
\end{equation}
When $K$ is held approximately constant by the budget ratio, attended
aggregation scales as $\mathcal{O}(NK)$ rather than dense $\mathcal{O}(N^{2})$.
We do \emph{not} claim that the entire forward pass is strictly linear in $N$:
radius candidate construction remains $\mathcal{O}(Nkd)$ as in other geometric
graph methods.
The practical claim is that \textbf{activated communication density}
decreases as $\eta_N=\mathcal{O}(K/N)$ under bounded $K$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:density}; Fig.~\ref{fig:density-scale}).

\noindent
Pairwise distance masking remains $\mathcal{O}(N^{2})$ and is shared by all
geometric graph methods; the practical gain of AC-DSGF is in
\emph{attended} edges and transmitted soft mass under $K\ll N$.
Fig.~\ref{fig:density-scale} illustrates communication density versus team size.
Table~\ref{tab:compute} reports CPU inference at $N{=}16$ alongside
Table~\ref{tab:main} Comm: the gate adds modest latency while cutting soft
mass by nearly two orders of magnitude versus radius-support DSGF/GAT.
